How We Will Deliver: Relief, Recovery, Reform

Relief empowers people to participate in recovery. Recovery powers reform. Reform locks in progress.

Relief

Immediate Help

  1. Remove the 2.5% Health & Security Levy to immediately reduce cost-of-living pressure.
  2. Reduce fuel prices to ease transportation and household expenses.
  3. Provide free tertiary education at Sir Arthur Lewis Community College.
  4. Strengthen national security by reintroducing border control operations and expanding the K9 unit.
  5. Deliver targeted financial support, including pensions and assistance for banana farmers, and expand National Health Insurance coverage.

Recovery

Building for Growth

  1. Upgrade national infrastructure — roads, bridges, water systems, and connectivity.
  2. Expand access to quality healthcare and modernise medical services.
  3. Strengthen education, skills training, and youth opportunities.
  4. Boost innovation and entrepreneurship across key sectors: agriculture, tourism, construction, digital, creative industries.
  5. Build a modern caring economy that supports caregivers, families, and vulnerable groups.

Reform

Fixing the System

  1. Strengthen democratic institutions to ensure independence and accountability.
  2. Modernise the public service through technology, efficiency, and performance standards.
  3. Update outdated laws and regulations to align with global best practices.
  4. Institutionalise citizen participation and improve transparency through open data and public reporting.
  5. Enforce strong anti-corruption and procurement reforms to build an ethical, efficient, and professional governance culture.
